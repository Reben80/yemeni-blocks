Play

Pick a brick, then tap a square. The brick starts on that square, and shifts inward if it would hang off the edge. Turn it by tapping it again in the tray, pressing R, right-clicking the board, or using Turn brick. Tap a placed brick to pick it up. A dot means the brick came with the card.

Keyboard: Tab to the board, arrows to move, Enter to place. 1 2 3 pick a length. Alt and an arrow key change the card.

Test variant: one placement fills all four colours.

Stuck?

Rules

  1. Use all 32 bricks and cover every square.
  2. Each colour forms one connected shape.
  3. Turn red a quarter turn clockwise around the centre: it lands exactly on yellow, brick for brick. Yellow lands on blue, blue on white, white on red.
  4. Each outlined region holds one colour.
